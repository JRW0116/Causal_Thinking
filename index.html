About this program

Say why, not just what

A benchmark tells a client where they stand. A causal claim tells them what to change — and what to stop doing. That is the product.

“Research at your fingertips that helps you understand what makes things true — not to believe that yesterday is going to tell you what's going to work tomorrow.”— Mike Carroll, COO Council

Three team commitments

  • Name the rung before you choose the verb.
  • Separate the surface effect from the cause.
  • Every study reports one thing we were wrong about.
